# JEBE ENTERPRISES — Wholesale Panel Sales & Distribution Website

A modern, high-performance, responsive business website crafted for **JEBE ENTERPRISES**, Wholesale Wall Panel Distributor & Bulk Stockist, featuring:
- **6 Wholesale Collections**: Fluted Panels, UV Sheets, Budget PVC Panels, Soffit Panels, Louvers (WPC), and Accessories.
- **Category-by-Category Presentation**: Organized catalog showcasing box packing, plank dimensions, specifications, and full-screen high-resolution image viewer.
- **Direct Phone & Warehouse Contact**: Direct sales hotline and depot address prominently visible on every page with **ZERO messaging buttons/links**.

---

## 6 Wholesale Product Collections

1. **Fluted Panels**:
   - Interior fluted wall panels in 2900mm (9.5ft) lengths.
   - Tongue-and-groove interlocking for living room feature walls, TV units, and commercial spaces.

2. **UV Sheets**:
   - High-gloss 8ft x 4ft UV marble and decorative stone composite sheets.
   - Crystal scratch-resistant UV coating for feature walls and TV backdrops.

3. **Budget PVC Panels**:
   - 10ft (H) x 1ft (W) interior PVC ceiling panels.
   - Supplied in wholesale packaging of 10 pieces per box.

4. **Soffit Panels**:
   - Wall and ceiling soffit systems combining realistic wood finishes with industrial-grade durability.
   - Standard 300mm width in 10ft and 13ft lengths.

5. **Louvers (WPC)**:
   - Heavy-duty Wood-Plastic Composite fluted louvers.
   - Available in 4-Line (24mm thickness) and 8-Line (17mm thickness) profiles, 6" width x 9.5ft height.

6. **Accessories**:
   - Hardware accessories for panel installation: Heavy-Duty Panel Adhesive, Concealed Fastening Clamps, H Trims, L Trims, and U Trims.
   - Wholesale packaging: Depends on the order.
